# Vehicle Health Center

A read-only summary of the health information already available to iDrive 7:

- engine coolant and oil temperatures, electronic oil level and 12 V battery condition;
- all four tyre pressures, targets and temperatures;
- BMW Condition Based Service requirements;
- active Check Control count and a current-fault scan using UDS service `19`;
- DPF soot, ash and CBS remaining distance on verified G-series diesel DDE variants.

No clear, reset, coding, calibration or actuator command is present. Unknown or
stale values remain blank instead of being guessed.
